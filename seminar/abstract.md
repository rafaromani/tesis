# How much does an AI need to think to answer our questions?

Transformers are the building blocks of modern AI. In recent years, some research has been conducted with the intention to formally analyze the expressive power of these types of models. Obtaining representative results is not an easy task, as it is unclear how to formalize transformers.

In this talk, I will present a known formalization of transformers and the limits of their computational power when restricting their Chain of Thought. These bounds are obtained using circuit theory.

Next, I will present a new construction that adds a key component missing from the previous formalization: randomness. I will then adapt the known results to the probabilistic version.
