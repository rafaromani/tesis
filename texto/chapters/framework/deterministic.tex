                % Deterministic transformer %
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{definition}[Deterministic transformer]
    We call a transformer deterministic when the token selector is the deterministic selector.
    We say that a family of deterministic transformers $\{\TF_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ recognizes a language $\gL$ when: 
    \[
    \alpha \in \gL \iff \TF_{|\alpha|}^*(\alpha) = \qaccept
    \]    
\end{definition}
