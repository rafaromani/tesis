The main objective of this thesis is to expand on the state-of-the-art bounds for transformer expressiveness.
We adapt \citet{toyota}'s results from deterministic transformers to probabilistic transformers.
Their main results imply that $\CoT[\poly(n), \log(n)] = \ppoly$.
This says that the set of languages recognizable by deterministic transformers with  embedding-dimension $\log(n)$ and doing $\poly(n)$ steps of $\CoT$ coincide with the ones recognizable by circuits of polynomial size.
