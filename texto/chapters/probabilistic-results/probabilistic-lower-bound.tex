Building upon Theorem \ref{sim-circ-by-det-transf}, we prove that constant-depth, constant-precision probabilistic transformers using $T$ steps of $\CoT$ with $O(\log(n))$ embedding size can simulate a randomized circuit of size $T$.

\begin{theorem}\label{sim-circ-by-prob-transf}
    Any family of randomized Boolean circuits of polynomial size $T(n)$ can be simulated by a family of constant-depth, constant-precision probabilistic transformers using $T(n)$ steps of $\CoT$ with $O(\log(n))$ embedding size 
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}\footnote{See Appendix \ref{appendix:prob-lower-bound} for the complete construction.}
    Let $\{C_n\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ be a family of randomized circuits of polynomial size.
    We construct a family of probabilistic transformers $\{\TF_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ such that each $\TF_n$ simulates $C_n$.
    As Definition \ref{def:accept-random-circ} states, a randomized circuit $C_n$ accepts $\alpha$ when $\Pr_{r_1 \dots r_{f(n)}}[C'_n(\alpha, r) = 1] > 2/3$, that is, the deterministic circuit $C'_n$ accepts $(\alpha, r)$ for $2/3$ of the possible values of $r$.
    Our construction first generates $r$ at random with one step of $\CoT$ per bit of $r$, and then simulates the deterministic circuit $C'$ with input $\alpha r$ (Theorem \ref{sim-circ-by-det-transf} and Theorem \ref{theo:prob-sim-det}).
\end{proofsketch}


Theorem \ref{sim-circ-by-prob-transf} can also be phrased with complexity classes as:

\begin{theorem}\label{prob-lower-bound}
    For any polynomial function $T:\mathbb{N}^+\to \mathbb{N}^+$, $\BPSIZE(T(n)) \subseteq \RCoT [T(n), \log(n)]$.
    In particular, $\BPppoly \subseteq \RCoT[\poly(n), \log(n)]$.
\end{theorem}
