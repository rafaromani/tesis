\chapter{Main results}

In this chapter, we present our results on probabilistic transformers.
We show that probabilistic transformers are more powerful than deterministic ones, as they are capable of simulating them.
We prove that the probabilistic classes support error reduction by means of a Monte Carlo simulation.
Furthermore, we also demonstrate that $\RCoT$ classes are closed under boolean operations.

Finally, we prove upper and lower bounds on the expressive power of probabilistic transformers.
These theorems are extensions of \cite{toyota}'s results for deterministic transformers.
