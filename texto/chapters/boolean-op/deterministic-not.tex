\begin{theorem}
    $\gL \in \CoTtndn \implies \gL^c \in \CoTtndn$
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch of the proof]\footnote{See Appendix \ref{appendix:det-not} for the complete proof.}
    Let $\gL \in \CoTtndn$. 
Then there is a family of transformers $\{\TF_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of embedding size $d(n)$ that after $T(n)$ steps of $\CoT$ prints $\qaccept$ or $\qreject$ depending on if the input word is in the language.
For each transformer in the family, we construct a corresponding transformer that behaves identically (internally and externally) but prints the flipped decision token as represented in Figure \ref{fig:not}.
\end{proof}
